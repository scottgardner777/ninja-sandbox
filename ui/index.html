The sample hits your front door. Watch it detonate. Then take it apart.

The Raz0r Sensor reads your edge and pulls the dropper URLs attackers throw at it, so the malware lands here in situ. Reverse it: structure, imports, strings, syscall timeline, process tree, files, C2 ranking, pcap, ATT&CK. Then Deep Scan it with a frontier model sitting next to you. No VM. No cloud. Your box.
